## Default Permission

Check and use Capri's local model, cancel a response, and clear its in-memory conversation.

- `allow-check-availability`
- `allow-respond`
- `allow-agent-step`
- `allow-reset`
- `allow-cancel`
- `allow-results-list`
- `allow-results-acknowledge`
- `allow-notification-status`
- `allow-notification-configure`
